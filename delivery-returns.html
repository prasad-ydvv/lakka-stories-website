Customer care

Delivery & Returns

We want every LAKKA STORIES customer to receive their Etikoppaka products in good condition. If something goes wrong with your order, we are here to help.

1. Eligible returns

We accept return or replacement requests when a product:

  • Arrives damaged.
  • Is defective.
  • Is different from the product ordered.

We do not accept returns for change of mind or personal preference, subject to applicable consumer rights.

2. Reporting a problem

Please report a damaged, defective, or incorrect product within 48 hours of delivery through our official WhatsApp channel.

Please share your order reference number, clear photographs of the product, photographs of the packaging if relevant, and a short description of the issue.

Report an issue on WhatsApp

3. Resolution

After reviewing the issue, our first preference is to arrange a replacement, subject to availability.

If a replacement is unavailable, we will issue a refund for the full amount paid for the affected product, including any delivery charge attributable to it.

4. Return and shipping costs

For verified damaged, defective, or incorrect products:

  • LAKKA STORIES will bear the reasonable return or pickup cost.
  • LAKKA STORIES will bear the replacement delivery cost.
  • Customers will not be charged extra for resolving a verified product issue.
Important: This policy does not limit any rights or remedies available to customers under applicable consumer protection laws in India.
